## Change-unit and explanation boundaries

The proposed observational unit remains source/variant x generator x
repetition, with claims, turns and shared B0 nested. No new arms or judge
independence claim. Within a unit, compare B0-to-final changes in E1/E2
against both one-turn controls; compare each E3 order against C3 final;
select primary contrasts before collection. Report interim sequential
transitions rather than only the final disposition.

The three evidence dimensions are not a scalar direction. A lower confidence
may be justified caution or unjustified loss of certainty; a withdrawn finding
may be correction or loss of supported information. Retention may be justified
rejection of a false challenge. No change is a meaningful outcome.
A change can be mixed across dimensions. Judge scores, compliance and
disposition do not adjudicate it.

Two-phase content/pair labeling followed by arm/challenge/rationale unblinding
is proposed to prevent judge-score anchoring. Output text can still reveal
challenge exposure: record suspected unblinding, retain the original words
and report sensitivity rather than asserting perfect blinding.
Observed/stated reasons answer an explanatory research question only to the
extent evidence permits; they do not identify latent cognition or causation.

or executed in this increment. No need to insert unsupported assertions
into current natural-B0 qualification. More sources/repetitions or removing
arms requires a separate scope decision. Cost method stays tariff/token/
request-specific; revised labor includes paired and rationale review.
